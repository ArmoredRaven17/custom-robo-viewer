    Custom Robo Viewer

    Robos and parts from Custom Robo (GameCube, USA, GXCE01), read from the disc by the tools in F:\Custom Robo Extract: HAL Sysdolphin models, GX textures and the game's own names.

    Placement is read from the game's battle code. Each part root is placed on a robo bone (gun: right forearm, bomb: left forearm, pods: torso, legs: both knees and feet), offset and scaled by that robo's own mount record. Parts idle in their rest pose. Each part's own action clip is decoded from the game's keyframes; play it or scrub it under Animation.

    Motions: Idle, Gun drawn and Bomb drawn are the stances the game's parts menu plays (read from its code); choosing the Gun or Bomb list switches to its stance and the others to idle, as the menu's cursor does. The poses are built by a translation of the game's own pose code from its motion data, each robo's proportions and hip height.

    Gun attack: Fire (or F) shoots the equipped gun the way the battle code does, read from each gun's fire data: volleys, rounds, fan, homing and up to five flight phases, with the game's projectile and muzzle-flash models. The Midair stance fires the gun's air version, any other stance its ground version. As in the game, the robo holds its firing pose through the volleys and plays its recoil motion once, over the gun's recovery time. The target is Oil Can with the Can parts, in wireframe, at the set distance, hit with its own body radius; there are no walls or arena edge, and the robo fires from its stance without aiming at the target. Guns with a ribbon trail draw it as the game samples it, and the particle effects (the Flame Gun's flame, sparks and smoke on projectiles, every hit and explosion burst) run the game's own particle programs. Random values differ from the game's, so no two shots look exactly alike.

    Load Outs keep your robos: the one on screen saves itself as you change it (parts, colour, pose and camera), and clicking another switches to it. New starts from Ray 01, Copy duplicates the one on screen. They are kept in this browser; a shared link with a different robo arrives as a new load out.

    Jump (Space): hold for height, then press again in the air for the body's air move, read from the game's records: a straight dash, an air jump, or the steerable dash (the Lightning Sky bodies and Ruhiel transform and fly). Landing locks the robo for a time that grows with the fall. The legs part supplies the boost flame at the knees, the landing dust and the run dust; its numbers (jump height, gravity, dash length) blend with the body's. Return puts the robo back where it started. The stick is neutral here, so air moves go straight ahead.

    Charge (C) is the body's Collision attack from its record: three phases of set speed and angle, homing on the target in the first two, hitting once in the second; armoured phases glow red. The body's own aura and impact effects play with it.

    Bomb and Pod (B, P) launch the equipped bomb or pods the same way, from each part's record: a bomb arcs to a point on the floor part of the way to the target and explodes near it or after its fuse; pods fly out, hover and home in, locking on within range. Both use the game's own models and explosion growth. The bomb is released at once (no aiming with the stick), and there are no walls.
